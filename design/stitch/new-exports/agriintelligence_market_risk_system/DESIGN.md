## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the convergence of high-precision agricultural intelligence, institutional fintech clarity, and grounded rural usability. It delivers actionable certainty to agrarian producers and market aggregators, transforming complex commodity volatility into lucid sell, hold, and hedge decisions. 

The aesthetic is characterized as **Agri-FinTech Precision**: a fusion of clean institutional minimalism and tactile modern dashboard architecture. The visual language conveys generational agricultural authority through deep chlorophyll-inspired greens, paired with energetic data accents and high-contrast numerical surfaces. The UI evokes strategic control, high reliability, and forward-looking clarity, avoiding rural clichés in favor of modern, institutional-grade analytics calibrated for field resilience, direct sunlight legibility, and multilingual workflows.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative agrarian foundation layered with high-visibility market telemetry.

- **Primary Canvas & Surfaces**: Base canvas utilizes `#F8FAF8`, an off-white that prevents field glare. Elevated analytical containers use tinted surfaces: `#F0FDF4` for base cards and `#ECFDF5` for highlighted intelligence pods. Neutral borders utilize translucent emerald strokes (`rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.08)` to `rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.14)`).
- **Primary & Secondary**: Deep Forest Green (`#166534`) acts as the anchor for structural chrome, navigation, headers, and core interactive frames. Vibrant Emerald Green (`#10B981`) serves as the active signal for positive price action, AI model validation, and confirmation states.
- **Strategic Accents**: Electric Lime (`#84CC16`) is reserved for high-impact AI highlights and live predictive nodes. Sky Blue (`#0284C7`) designates logistics, multi-mandi transport arbitration, and liquidity markers.
- **Risk & Decision States**: Strictly semantic. Low Risk / Bullish trends use `#10B981`; Medium Risk / Holding patterns use Harvest Amber (`#F59E0B`); High Risk / Liquidate warnings command Crimson Red (`#EF4444`).
- **Typography Contrast**: Primary data metrics and labels render in slate-forest neutral darks (`#0F291E` and `#1E3A2F`), guaranteeing WCAG AAA compliance against tinted cards under outdoor sunlight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes rapid scannability, numerical prominence, and non-Latin glyph fidelity. Plus Jakarta Sans handles human-centric communication and structural headlines with contemporary warmth. Inter provides clinical, tabular metrics and compact labels to prevent misinterpretation of numbers (weights, rates, moisture percentages, net returns).

- **Multilingual Support**: All font declarations must feature fallbacks configured for Noto Sans Devanagari to support Hindi (हिंदी) and Marathi (मराठी). Line heights are set 8–12% taller than standard English baselines on body styles to avoid clipping compound conjuncts and vowel matras.
- **Metric Formatting**: Prices (₹/Quintal), percentage yield forecasts, and confidence scores always leverage Inter's tabular figures (`tnum`) and slashed zeros to ensure aligned comparison columns in analytical matrices.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a responsive, density-aware fluid grid structured around 12 columns on desktop (breakpoints above 1024px), 8 columns on tablet (640px to 1023px), and 4 columns on mobile (under 640px).

- **Rhythm & Touch Scale**: The rhythm follows an intentional 8pt grid with 4pt half-steps for micro-alignments. Interactive touch targets on mobile and field views enforce a strict minimum boundary of 48×48px with `space-sm` separation to prevent mis-taps under rough operating environments.
- **Desktop Architecture**: Employs a fixed-width left navigation rail (260px) paired with a responsive fluid dashboard canvas maxing out at 1600px, flanked by `margin` gutters to retain peripheral focus.
- **Mobile Reflow**: On mobile breakpoints, multi-column analytical panels stack into vertically scrollable decision cards. Horizontal carousels are limited strictly to peer mandi pricing comparisons and crop timeline checkpoints.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through an atmospheric layering model rather than arbitrary drop shadows. It relies on ambient environmental illumination reflecting through a clean canopy:

- **Level 0 (Floor)**: Canvas background (`#F8FAF8`), flat with no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card Baseline)**: Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` or `#F0FDF4`) set with a 1px structural outline of `rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.08)` and an atmospheric ambient shadow: `0px 2px 8px -2px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.04), 0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.06)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive & Hover Cards)**: Slightly amplified elevation: `0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.06), 0px 16px 32px -6px rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.10)`. The stroke shifts to `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Decision Sheets & Drawers)**: Heavy focus elevation: `0px 24px 48px -12px rgba(15, 41, 30, 0.20)` supplemented with a background backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(15, 41, 30, 0.40)`).

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances organic agrarian curves with structured dashboard discipline:

- **Cards & Data Modules**: Standardize on `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px to 1.5rem / 24px based on viewport scale). This creates an approachable yet orderly frame for high-density charts and complex tables.
- **Action Elements & Inputs**: Standard form inputs, dropdown selectors, and secondary controls use `rounded-xl` (0.75rem / 12px) to provide defined, tap-friendly zones.
- **Status, AI Badges & Pills**: Tags, market status flags, and AI confidence indicators strictly use full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to immediately differentiate non-actionable status metadata from structural containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Confirm Sell Order", "Generate Forecast")**: Solid Deep Forest Green (`#166534`) background, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded-xl, with an internal vertical padding of `space-sm` (12px) and horizontal `space-lg` (24px). Subtle active scale animation (0.98).
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button with 1.5px solid `#166534`, transparent background, text `#166534`.
- **AI Recommendation Triggers**: Vibrant Emerald (`#10B981`) text on an `#ECFDF5` container with an Electric Lime (`#84CC16`) focus-glow state.

### Chips & Badges
- Built as pill shapes (`rounded-full`) utilizing `label-sm` or `label-xs` typography.
- **Risk Indicator Pills**:
  - *Low Risk*: `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, with a 6px solid `#10B981` leading pulse dot.
  - *Medium Risk*: `#FFFBEB` background, `#92400E` text, with a 6px solid `#F59E0B` leading dot.
  - *High Risk*: `#FEF2F2` background, `#991B1B` text, with a 6px solid `#EF4444` leading dot.
- **AI Confidence Score Chip**: Dark forest capsule (`#166534`) with text `#FFFFFF` displaying dynamic rating (e.g., `94% AI Accuracy`) paired with an `#84CC16` accent glyph.

### Cards (Decision & Intelligence Pods)
- Encapsulated in `rounded-2xl` containers with base `#FFFFFF` or `#F0FDF4`. Outlined with a continuous 1px stroke of `border-emerald-900/10`.
- Cards segment into distinct zones: Header (Metric Title & Multi-Crop Badge), Primary Metric Anchor (Tabular Inter 32px font), Trend Differential Indicator (+₹140/Qtl), and Action/Risk Recommendation footer.

### Form Inputs & Multi-Select Dropdowns
- Height of 48px to accommodate one-handed field entry. Surfaces use white with 1.5px border in `rgba(22, 101, 52, 0.15)`. Focus ring shifts cleanly to `#10B981` with a 3px soft outer ring tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)`). Labels sit persistently above the input in `label-sm` with multilingual subtitle indicators.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Sized at 20×20px with a 48×48px transparent touch wrapper. Selected states fill with `#166534` housing a crisp `#FFFFFF` check/dot mark.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Mandi Arbitrage Card**: Split-column component displaying local vs. regional destination APMC market rates, displaying transport freight cost deductions and estimated net realization delta.
- **Commodity Holding Horizon Gauge**: A multi-step risk bar indicating projected returns over 15, 30, and 60 days, colored dynamically via semantic risk gradients (Green to Crimson).
